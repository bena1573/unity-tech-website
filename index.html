Questions

Frequently asked.

How long does a typical project take?

Most web projects ship in 4–8 weeks; mobile apps and AI products typically run 8–14 weeks depending on scope.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes — roughly half our clients are pre-seed to Series A. We scope tightly so you ship an MVP without overbuilding.

What does support after launch look like?

Every project includes a support window post-launch, and ongoing retainers are available for teams who want a long-term partner.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Absolutely. We start with a technical audit so you know exactly what you're inheriting before we touch a line of code.
